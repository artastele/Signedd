## Required Sample Assessment Data

Prepare one assessment record showing:

- Communication strengths and needs
- Academic baseline
- Functional performance notes
- Recommended accommodations
- Teacher assessment remarks
- Assessment status ready for IEP meeting

## Required IEP / PDSP Demo Data

Prepare one IEP meeting and related documents:

- Meeting schedule with parent, SPED teacher, guidance, principal, and relevant staff
- PDSP demo entries for learner needs, services, responsible persons, and timelines
- Signed PDSP demo upload file
- IEP goals for communication, reading, numeracy, and social participation
- Signatories for parent, SPED teacher, guidance, principal, and learner/guardian where applicable
- One lesson plan linked to the IEP
- One learner activity assigned from the lesson plan
- One learner submission with a completed/graded result

## Manual Demo Flow Checklist

- Parent registers.
- Parent selects parent role.
- Parent submits enrollment for Juan D. Santos.
- SPED teacher reviews and verifies enrollment.
- Student record is created/available.
- SPED teacher conducts assessment.
- IEP meeting is scheduled.
- PDSP is prepared.
- Signed PDSP document is uploaded/marked signed.
- IEP is created from the learner record and assessment context.
- IEP is sent for required signing.
- Required signatories complete signing.
- SPED teacher creates or assigns a lesson/activity.
- Learner logs in and completes the activity.
- SPED teacher reviews/grades the submission.
- Parent and teacher progress views show activity progress.

No database seeding is included in this plan.
